Vallisnieri–Stancari Correspondence

A digital scholarly edition of 30 letters (1707–1709) from Antonio Vallisnieri to Francesco Vittorio Stancari. A corpus of early eighteenth-century Italian history of science, extracted and annotated using knowledge graph techniques and artificial intelligence.

The corpus

The letters document exchanges of biological specimens, scientific observations, texts and networks of correspondents. Vallisnieri uses Stancari as a remote observer in Bologna, while writing from Padua. The network includes correspondents in Venice, Naples, Rome, Amsterdam and London.
